Preliminary Results out for the Karnataka Rajyotsava Bird Count 2026

30 October to 2 November 2026

The Karnataka Rajyotsava Bird Count (KRBC) ran for four days. Anyone could join by watching birds for at least 15 minutes and uploading a complete checklist to eBird. Here is what came in.

Seen everywhere, and seen in one place

52 species were recorded in all 30 eBird regions. These are Karnataka’s everyday birds. 32 species were recorded in only one district. Udupi and Uttara Kannada had 10 each. Most of these are shorebirds, wetland birds and a few rare visitors.

Some notable singles included Bar-tailed Godwit, Besra, Black Biter, Common Tern, Eurasian Hobby, Eurasian Oystercatcher, Pied Avocet, Siberian Stonechat, Blyth’s Swift, and many others. The final report may update these lists.

The under-birded districts

Before the count, these 17 districts had fewer than 10,000 complete checklists on eBird. Together they sent in 3,202 checklists, which is 38.9% of all checklists in the count. Tumakuru (686) and Hassan (442) led the count. Tumakuru and Hassan were also the two most under-birded districts.
