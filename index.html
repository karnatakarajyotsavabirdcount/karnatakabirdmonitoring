Karnataka’s Avifauna

From the rainforests of the Western Ghats to coastal wetlands, dry scrublands, farmlands, lakes, and city parks, Karnataka supports an extraordinary diversity of birds. The Karnataka Bird Monitoring Network connects people and places through citizen science.

548+ Species

Karnataka supports one of India’s richest avifaunas, including many endemic and threatened species.

38 Endemics

The state hosts 38 of India’s 79 endemic bird species, making it a priority landscape for conservation.

Citizen Science

Every checklist, photograph, and field observation helps build a clearer picture of Karnataka’s birds.

Karnataka’s Bird Habitats

Western Ghats Forests

Evergreen and moist deciduous forests support hornbills, barbets, woodpeckers, flycatchers, and forest understory birds.

Wetlands and Rivers

Lakes, tanks, reservoirs, and river systems provide vital habitat for herons, storks, pelicans, cormorants, and migratory waterbirds.

Grasslands and Farmland

Open landscapes support larks, bush quail, lapwings, rollers, francolins, and open-country raptors.

Urban Green Spaces

Parks, lakes, gardens, and campus greenery offer accessible places for everyday birdwatching and monitoring.
