About Us

The Karnataka Bird Monitoring Network is a community-led initiative that brings together birdwatchers, naturalists, researchers, students, educators, forest staff, and local communities to document and conserve Karnataka’s birdlife.

Our Vision

A Karnataka where bird diversity is documented, understood, valued, and conserved for future generations.

Our Mission

To enable participatory bird monitoring, build birdwatching capacity, generate reliable observations, and support evidence-based conservation across Karnataka.

What We Do

  • Organise bird counts, nature walks, surveys, and workshops.
  • Encourage people to submit complete checklists through eBird.
  • Share field stories, monitoring updates, and conservation articles.
  • Build partnerships with birdwatching groups, institutions, and communities.
  • Promote responsible, ethical, and inclusive birdwatching.

Get Involved

You can participate in an event, submit bird observations, write a field story, volunteer for a survey, or collaborate with the network.
